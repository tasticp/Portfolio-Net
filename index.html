01 / Work log

Ten weeks of hands-on work.

The work moved between the workshop, engineering software and documentation. Each task gave me a different view of how the learning systems are assembled, maintained and prepared for use.

Weeks 1–2 / 01

Conveyor assembly & testing

Assembled and tested conveyor sections, checked motor operation and became familiar with the MPS and CP Lab equipment.

Weeks 2–3 / 02

3D modelling & printing

Worked on a cap model and learned the 3D-printing workflow, checking the physical result against the intended design.

Weeks 3–5 / 03

Station repairs & maintenance

Helped investigate faults and restore stations for demonstrations and classes, including belt work and a drill-station replacement.

Weeks 3–6 / 04

Technical documentation

Prepared two manuals: a task-focused guide for daily work and a user manual for Ngee Ann Polytechnic staff and students.

Weeks 6–8 / 05

Air supply & station support

Worked on the air supply for the stations and supported setup and maintenance tasks, following safe isolation and pressure-release steps.

Weeks 9–10 / 06

PLC tags & test code

Started creating PLC tags and test code in Siemens TIA Portal V15.1, developing familiarity with project structure and signal behaviour.

03 / Learning & reflection

A better process matters as much as the fix.

My mechatronics background gave me a starting point. The internship has shown me the importance of understanding the actual machine, its signals, its users and its operating conditions.

01 / Troubleshoot systematically

Isolate faults one part at a time and check simple causes before assuming a major failure.

02 / Work safely

Prioritise safe steps such as isolating power and releasing pressure before maintenance.

03 / Document for the reader

Use clear steps, consistent names and checklists so another person can repeat the task.

Current development area

PLC programming & troubleshooting

PLC programming is less familiar to me than the mechanical work. I am working on my confidence with ladder logic, understanding how inputs and outputs control equipment, and troubleshooting more independently.
